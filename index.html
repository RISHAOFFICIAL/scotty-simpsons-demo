Seventy-six years. Three owners. One recipe.

James "Scotty" Simpson opened the doors at 22200 Fenkell in 1950, serving fish and chips to returning GIs who had developed a taste for the British staple while stationed in London.

Scotty brokered a deal with an Eastern Market vendor to fly fresh-caught cod in from Halifax, Nova Scotia every morning — packed in ice, never frozen. That relationship stands to this day.

Harry Barber started here as a busboy and dishwasher on his first day of high school in 1966. He bought the restaurant in 2002 and still runs the fryer himself. In 76 years, only three owners have guided Scotty's — and the secret batter recipe has never changed.

Named Best Fish and Chips in Detroit by Hour Detroit magazine and Best Seafood in Metro Detroit by Channel 4. Cozymeal named our fish and chips the #1 food in Detroit.
