THE STORY

Three owners. One recipe. Seventy-six years.

James "Scotty" Simpson opened the doors in 1950, serving fish and chips to GIs coming home from the war. He made a deal at Eastern Market to fly fresh cod in from Halifax, Nova Scotia every morning. Never frozen. That deal still stands.

The batter recipe hasn't changed since day one. Harry Barber started here as a busboy and dishwasher in 1966. He bought the place in 2002. He's still the head cook. Still the greeter. Still the guy who'll ask how your mother is doing.

Only three owners in 76 years. That's not a business. That's a family.
