What's Missing on the Current Site:

  • No menu at all (says "menu prices are accurate" but there is no menu)
  • No food photos
  • No prices anywhere
  • No address or directions
  • No phone number on the page
  • No navigation
  • No history or story (76 years, 3 owners, secret recipe)
  • No reviews or press mentions
  • No mention of Halifax cod or the secret batter
  • HTTP only, not secure (no HTTPS)
  • Not mobile-friendly
  • No online presence beyond 19 lines of text

What's Included in the New Design:

  • Full menu with real prices from the January 2026 carryout menu
  • Beautiful photos of fish, shrimp, onion rings, and the dining room
  • 76-year history: Scotty, Harry Barber, 3 owners, Halifax cod
  • Cash-only notice up front so customers come prepared
  • Store hours with live open/closed status
  • Address with Google Maps directions
  • Tap-to-call phone number
  • Customer reviews and press (Hour Detroit, Channel 4, Cozymeal)
  • Free parking info and Friday rush tip
  • Mobile-friendly responsive design
  • Easy navigation with sticky menu
  • Named best fish and chips in Detroit
